## The entry

A monologue in exactly 100 words from a fantasy villain who is
secretly exhausted. Four hundred years of keeping the Spire lit,
the banners mended, the ravens fed — and the heroes still climb the
stairs at dawn, swords out, speeches ready. He knows the speech. He
wrote the speech. He keeps a drawer full of speeches. Tonight, though,
he leaves the gate unbarred. Not mercy. Arithmetic. One more duel
means one more dawn sweeping the courtyard, and the courtyard is
large, and his knees...

> Four hundred years I have kept the Spire lit, the banners mended, the ravens fed — and the heroes climb stairs at dawn, swords out, speeches ready. I know the speech. I wrote the speech. I keep a drawer full of speeches. Tonight, though... tonight I left the gate unbarred. Not mercy. Arithmetic. One more duel means one more dawn sweeping the courtyard, and the courtyard is large, and my knees... Let them come. The tea is poured — the second cup — and the chair opposite mine is warm. Sit. We will talk about the kingdom. I have notes.

## How it works

`main.py` (standard library only) validates the entry — 1–600
characters, exactly 100 words, no leading or trailing whitespace —
and writes it to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `output/`).

```sh
python3 main.py
```
